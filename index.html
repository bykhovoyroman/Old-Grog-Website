Broad Street · Menasha, Wisconsin

Part of the neighborhood

Old walls.
Good stories.
Great company.

Our story starts long before the first drink was poured. Built in 1890, this Broad Street building has been a livery, a grocery and a tavern, among other chapters in its history.

The Old Grog opened in 1973. Inside, beams from the Menasha Wooden Ware Factory and historic photographs connect the room to the city around it. Today, it's still a place to settle in, share a meal and feel at home.
